Configuration

Settings.

Club-wide configuration. Changes take effect immediately and are written to the audit log.

GeneralClub identity & branding ELO & RankingScoring formulas MatchesSubmission & disputes NotificationsEmail & push AdminsRoles & access

General

The basic identity of your club. These values appear on player-facing pages and exported PDFs.

Club Name
Shown in headers, email signatures, and the player app.
Short Code
Used in match IDs and URLs. Three letters, uppercase.
Tagline
A one-line descriptor displayed below the logo.
Time Zone
All match times and audit logs use this zone.
Public Visibility
When on, the leaderboard is viewable without an account.

Danger Zone

Reset all ELO ratings

Every player drops to the season starting value. Match history is preserved. Cannot be undone.

Wipe match history

Removes every match record from the database. ELO snapshots remain. Cannot be undone.

Delete club

Permanently removes this club and all associated data, including audit logs. Cannot be undone.

ELO & Ranking

The math behind the ladder. Be cautious — changes affect all future matches and dispute outcomes.

Starting ELO
Assigned to all new players on approval.
K-Factor
How aggressively rating moves per match. Higher = more volatile.
ELO Floor
No player can drop below this value, regardless of losses.
Provisional Period
Number of matches a new player plays at 2× K-factor.
Soft Reset Between Seasons
At season rollover, regress every rating 50% toward the starting ELO.
Decay for Inactive Players
Lose 5 ELO per week after 30 days of no recorded matches.

Matches

Submission rules, dispute windows, and what counts as a valid result.

Submission Window
A match must be submitted within this many hours of being played.
Dispute Window
After a result is posted, opponents have this long to dispute.
Require Both Players Confirm
Match doesn't count until both submit matching scores.
Allow Score Format
Best-of-three sets to 21, or single game to 21.
No-Show Penalty
ELO deduction for missing a scheduled match without notice.
Cross-Skill Matches
When on, players more than 200 ELO apart can still play ranked.
