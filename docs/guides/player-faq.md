# Player FAQ

Everything a member needs to know to use the app. Plain and friendly.

**App:** [badminton.polardev.org](https://badminton.polardev.org)

## Getting in

**How do I sign in?**
Two ways: enter your email and we'll send you a **6-digit code**, or tap **Sign in with Google**. (The code approach is used because some email systems auto-click login links and break them — a code always works.)

**Do I need to install anything?**
No, but you can. Open the site on your phone and choose "Add to Home Screen" — it then behaves like a real app, with its own icon.

## The ladder & your rating

**How does the rating work?**
It's an **ELO** system (like chess or ranked video games). Everyone starts at **400**. Win against a strong opponent and you gain more; lose to a weaker one and you drop more. The top of the club sits around **1300**.

**Why do I have two ratings?**
Singles and doubles are rated **separately** — being great at one doesn't inflate the other.

**What does "provisional" mean?**
For your first **8 matches** in a format, your rating is still finding its level, so it moves faster. After that it "locks in" and settles.

**Do all matches count the same?**
No — tournament and higher-stakes matches move your rating **more** than casual games.

## Playing matches

**How do I play a ranked match?**
1. **Issue a challenge** to another member (or accept one).
2. Play your match.
3. **Report the result** — the other player confirms it.
4. Both ratings update automatically once confirmed.

**A challenge I sent disappeared.**
Unanswered challenges **expire** after a while so they don't pile up. Just send a new one.

**Someone reported a wrong score.**
You can **dispute** a result. Admins review disputes and no-shows to keep the ladder fair.

## Sessions

**How do I attend a session?**
Browse the **Sessions** list, and **check in** from your phone. You can also add a session to your personal calendar in one tap. You'll get reminders so you don't forget.

**What's the difference between Competitive and Recreational?**
Sessions have a **track**. You'll see the ones that match your membership (plus any "all" sessions).

## Tournaments

**How do I join a tournament?**
Open the tournament, find the event you want, and **register** during its registration phase. On event day, **check in**. Brackets are **seeded by rating** so matches are as fair as possible.

## Notifications & privacy

**Can I get notifications?**
Yes — enable push notifications for reminders and challenge alerts.

**Can I hide from the public leaderboard?**
Yes — there's an option to be hidden from the public ranking. Ask an admin if you don't see it.

**Who can see my info?**
Your name and rating appear on the ladder (unless you opt out). Personal details (email, fees) are only visible to admins. See the [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy-policy.md).

## Appearance

**Dark mode?**
Yes — toggle it, and the app remembers your choice.
