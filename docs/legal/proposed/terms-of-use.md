## 1. The app and these Terms

SFU Badminton Club (the Club) is a student-led Sport Club within SFU Recreation.
The Club operates this app to help members manage club activities, including
sessions, tournaments, membership information, and a competitive ladder. These
Terms explain the rules for using the app. They sit alongside the Club's Code of
Conduct, SFU Recreation rules, applicable University policies, and the
requirements of any venue or event.

Access to Club activities depends on completing the Club's participation
requirements and any eligibility or event registration shown in the app.

## 2. Eligibility and account responsibility

The app is for current or prospective participants who meet the Club's eligibility
rules. The Club's activities may include SFU students, staff, and alumni. Some
activities may have additional registration, venue, capacity, age, or skill
requirements that will be shown before signup.

Members under 19 may take part with the parent or guardian permission described in
the Club's participation waiver. That permission is given by the parent or
guardian, not by the member.

Give accurate information when creating an account and keep it reasonably current.
Keep your sign-in method private. Tell us promptly at `exec@sfubadminton.com` if
you suspect someone has used your account without permission. Do not create an
account for another person or use another person's account without authorization.

## 3. Acceptable use

You must use the app for legitimate club purposes. You must not:

- Fabricate or manipulate matches, ratings, attendance, eligibility, or fee status.
- Harass, threaten, discriminate against, or impersonate another person.
- Try to access another account, private member data, or administrative functions
  without authorization.
- Interfere with app security or availability, including through automated abuse
  or excessive scraping.
- Upload content that is unlawful, misleading, or unrelated to club activities.

The Code of Conduct applies to behaviour connected with the Club in the app and
club-related messages.

## 4. Ladder rules and corrections

Only genuine matches played under the Club's published ladder rules should be
submitted. Players are responsible for checking their reported result and raising
a mistake promptly. The Club may review scores, ask players for information,
correct or void a result, adjust a rating, and restrict ladder eligibility when
justified. Affected players will be told how to request a review at
`exec@sfubadminton.com`. They can contact `sport_clubs@sfu.ca` if the concern
involves club leadership.

Ratings and rankings are club competition tools. They may change when results are
corrected or the rating method is updated. The Club will explain a material change
to the rating method before applying it to future matches.

## 5. Sessions and tournaments

An event listing should show its date, location, capacity, eligibility, fee if any,
and cancellation or no-show rules before you register. Register only for places you
intend to use. If you cannot attend, cancel as soon as possible so a place can be
offered to someone else. Organizers may change or cancel an activity for safety,
facility, staffing, or other operational reasons and will notify registered
participants when reasonably possible.

Follow venue and Recreation staff instructions. Complete the Club's approved
participation form before joining a covered activity. The Club will tell you about
any additional requirement for a particular session or event before you register.

## 6. Fees and refunds

Any fee will be shown before you pay or register. SFU Recreation's published refund
policy applies to Sport Club fees handled by Recreation. It states that Sport Club
fee refunds require Recreation administration approval and that $10 Sport Club fees
are non-refundable. Additional club or event fees, if any, must have their own
terms shown before payment.

`[Confirm the Club's actual fee collection and refund process with Recreation
before publishing. The app tracks fee status but does not currently take online
payment, so the refund wording describes a process that does not run through the
app.]`

## 7. App content and member submissions

Members may submit scores, profile information, and other material needed for club
features. Submit only information you are entitled to provide and keep it accurate.
The Club may correct or remove app content that is inaccurate, abusive, unsafe, or
outside the app's purpose. Where content concerns another person, the Club will
handle it under the Privacy and Data Retention Policy.

## 8. Restrictions and decisions

The Club may temporarily restrict an app feature, account, or participation in a
Club activity while it addresses a credible misuse, safety, or security concern. It
will normally explain the concern and provide a chance to respond before a longer
club-level restriction. Decisions affecting SFU Recreation membership privileges
are handled through Recreation's conduct and discipline process.

## 9. Availability and updates

We work to keep the app available and records accurate, but outages, mistakes, or
changes may occur. Report an app problem to `exec@sfubadminton.com`. We may change
features or these Terms. We will notify users of material changes, show the new
version and effective date, and request renewed acceptance when appropriate before
continued use of affected features.

## 10. Privacy, participation forms, and contact

The Privacy and Data Retention Policy explains how app information is handled. The
Club's participation waiver is separate from these Terms, and photograph and video
consent is separate from both: it is optional, off unless you turn it on, and
withdrawable in Settings without affecting your participation. We will show any
additional activity-specific form before registration for the relevant event. These
Terms do not replace a waiver or waive anyone's legal rights.

Contact `exec@sfubadminton.com` for questions about the app or a club-level
decision; contact `sport_clubs@sfu.ca` for Recreation concerns.
