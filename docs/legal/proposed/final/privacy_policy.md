## 1. Who we are

SFU Badminton Club ("the Club", "we") operates a members' app for managing club activities: a competitive ladder, sessions, tournaments, and membership. This policy explains what personal information we collect and how we handle it, for members and for guests who are not members.

## 2. What we collect

From members:

- **Name and display name:** to identify you on the ladder, at sessions, and to other members.
- **Email address:** sign-in, reminders, and announcements.
- **Phone number** (optional): contact for sessions and events, if provided.
- **Match and rating data:** to run the ladder (scores, wins and losses, Elo rating).
- **Attendance data:** session and tournament check-ins.
- **Fee and payment status:** to track membership dues.
- **Match predictions:** a predicted chance of winning a singles or doubles match between members, worked out from match and rating data (see section 3).
- **Account and technical data:** login sessions and basic error reports.

We collect this when you sign up, use the app, play matches, and attend sessions. If you register for a tournament or a club event through a Google Form, your answers (including your email and any partner's) are sent to the app, matched to your account by email, used to enter you in the events you chose, and used to record your fee. For doubles, each player submits their own response naming the other, and you are paired only once both have.

From guests who are not members:

- **Guest waiver:** a guest who plays at a session signs the liability waiver online. We record the name and email address they give, that they confirmed they are 19 or older, the versions of the waiver and this policy they accepted, when they signed, and their browser type. We do not store their IP address: we keep only a scrambled code made from it, with a secret key, used to stop the form being abused.
- **External entrants:** a non-member may enter a tournament or a club event, either through its Google Form or, for a tournament, by an organiser entering their team. We record their name and, when they register through the form, their email address, any partner, the events they entered and their fee payment. We email the person who submitted the form a link to sign the guest waiver, to pass on to an external partner if they have one, and record whether each external entrant has signed it. Their results are never rated and never count towards anyone's rating or statistics.

## 3. How we use it

- To operate the ladder, sessions, tournaments, and membership.
- To send you sign-in codes, reminders, and club announcements.
- To keep the app secure and working (error monitoring, abuse prevention).
- To produce club statistics (e.g. standings, participation).
- To show **match predictions**: a club-run model estimates who is likely to win a singles or doubles match between members, from their match results and ratings. Predictions are shown in the app as estimates, and only to the members playing in that challenge. They never change anyone's rating, ranking or record, and no decision about you is made from them.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 4. Who can see what

- **Other members and the public:** your **name and rating** appear on the ladder and may appear on the public leaderboard, unless you opt out (you can hide yourself from the public leaderboard in Settings). A match prediction is shown only to the members playing in that challenge.
- **Executives:** club-activity data (sessions, tournaments, matches).
- **Admins:** the above **plus** sensitive data (email, phone, fee status, member records, guest waivers).
- **External entrants:** their name is shown on the pages of the tournament or event they entered. Their email, fee record and waiver status are seen only by admins and the event's organisers.
- **Approved Data API users:** the Club lets approved users, such as a club executive's prediction model or a partner building something for members, read club data through a keyed interface (the Data API). Each key is issued by the Club, limited to what it was granted, and can be revoked. It shows ratings, match results, standings, tournaments and the session schedule, with each member identified by a code that is different for every key. It **never** gives out your name, email, phone number or account ID, and never the names of guests or external teams. It covers only members who appear on the public leaderboard and have not asked to delete their account. A key may also be allowed to send match predictions back into the app.
- **Service providers:** we use third-party services to run the app: email delivery, error monitoring, sign-in, Google Forms for some tournament and event registrations, and our hosting, network and backup infrastructure. They process data only to provide their service. We do **not** run usage analytics: the software is present in the app's code but is switched off and collects nothing.

## 5. How we protect it

We use multiple safeguards, including secure login, role-based access, database-level access controls, encryption of off-site backups, and ongoing security review. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures.

## 6. Data retention

- **While you're a member:** we keep your information for as long as you have an account, to run the ladder, sessions, and membership.
- **Account deletion:** you can delete your account yourself in Settings. Your account is deactivated immediately and held for a **30-day** window, during which you can sign back in and restore it yourself. After 30 days, your account is permanently anonymized: your name, email, phone number, and profile photo are removed and your sign-in is deleted. This cannot be undone.
- **What remains after deletion:** match results, ratings, session attendance, and legal-acceptance records are kept under an anonymous identity ("Deleted Player") so the ladder's history and the club's records stay accurate.
- **Match predictions:** a new prediction for the same match replaces the old one. Predictions involving you stop being shown when you hide yourself from the public leaderboard or ask to delete your account, and are removed when your account is anonymized.
- **Backups:** encrypted off-site database backups are kept for **14 days** and then deleted automatically. A deleted account ages out of backups the same way.
- **Audit logs:** administrative audit logs (records of admin and executive actions) are kept indefinitely for accountability.
- **Legal acceptances:** records of your acceptance of the waiver, code of conduct, terms of use, and this policy are kept permanently as evidence of what was accepted, when, and under which version.
- **External entrants:** their entry and fee records, including their name and email, are kept as club records, like members' tournament and fee records. If they later join the club with the same email, their fee record follows them to their account.
- **Guest waivers:** a guest's waiver record is kept permanently, for the same reason: it is the Club's evidence that the waiver was signed. It is not joined to any account the guest opens later.
- **Attendance and reliability:** session attendance and reliability records are kept as club records.

## 7. Your rights

You may:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate information (most of it directly in Settings).
- **Delete** your account and personal information (see Data retention above for what is kept).
- **Opt out** of the public leaderboard. This also takes you out of the Data API and out of match predictions.
- **Withdraw** from notifications.

Three of these you can do yourself in Settings right now, without asking anyone: download everything we hold about you, delete your account, and change your visibility. For anything else, email `privacy@sfubadminton.com`.

Guests and external entrants have the same rights over the information we hold about them. Email `privacy@sfubadminton.com`.

## 8. Eligibility

The app is intended for SFU students and club members. Members under 19 need a parent or guardian's consent (see the Liability Waiver). Guests must be 19 or older to sign the guest waiver.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated to members and may require re-acceptance in the app. The version shown with this document reflects the current revision.
