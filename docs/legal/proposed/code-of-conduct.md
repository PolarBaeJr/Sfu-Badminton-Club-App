## 1. Purpose and scope

SFU Badminton Club (the Club) is a student-led Sport Club within SFU Recreation.
We want players of different backgrounds and skill levels to enjoy badminton in a
safe, welcoming, and competitive environment. This Code applies to members,
guests, volunteers, and club executives at sessions, tournaments, socials, and
other club activities. It also applies to conduct connected with the Club in the
app, email, messaging groups, and social media.

This Code adds club-specific rules for badminton and the ladder. Participants must
also follow SFU Recreation's Code of Conduct and Discipline Policy, applicable
University policies, and the rules of the venue or event. If a University or
Recreation rule governs a matter, the Club will follow that rule.

## 2. What we expect

Participants must:

- Treat teammates, opponents, staff, volunteers, executives, and visitors with
  courtesy and respect.
- Include new and less-experienced players. Do not ridicule anyone because of
  their skill level.
- Play safely and fairly, call scores honestly, and respect agreed match and
  tournament rules.
- Respect personal boundaries. Ask before giving physical guidance or making
  contact that is not part of ordinary play.
- Follow reasonable directions from club organizers and Recreation or venue staff,
  including safety and equipment instructions.
- Use courts, shuttles, and equipment responsibly; report damage or hazards
  promptly.
- Attend matches and events they have committed to, or give the organizer as much
  notice as possible if they cannot attend.
- Participate without being impaired by alcohol or drugs, and comply with
  Recreation rules on prohibited items.

## 3. Ladder and match integrity

The ladder depends on results that reflect matches actually played. Players must
submit accurate scores and must not invent results, use another person's account,
deliberately lose to manipulate a rating, arrange fake matches, collude to move
ratings, or pressure another player to report a false result.

If a result is disputed, the Club may ask both players for their accounts and check
available match records. A club executive who played in or has a close connection
to a disputed match should not decide that dispute alone. Depending on the facts,
the Club may correct a score or rating, void a match, or restrict ladder
eligibility. Affected players will be told the outcome and may ask for a review at
`exec@sfubadminton.com`. They may contact `sport_clubs@sfu.ca` if the concern
involves club leadership.

Repeated no-shows may affect access to challenges or events, but the Club will
consider reasonable explanations and communicate the applicable event rule before
imposing a restriction.

## 4. Conduct that is not allowed

The Club does not permit harassment, discrimination, bullying, stalking, threats,
intimidation, violence, sexual harassment, unwanted physical contact, retaliation
for a good-faith report, deliberate damage to property, or misuse of the app or
another person's information. This includes conduct in person and through
club-related digital channels. Conduct that violates applicable law or SFU or
Recreation policy is also prohibited.

## 5. Raising a concern

Report a club concern to `conduct@sfubadminton.com`. If the report concerns an
executive or you do not feel comfortable contacting the Club, contact SFU
Recreation at `sport_clubs@sfu.ca`. For an immediate threat, seek emergency
assistance or contact campus security.

Please include what happened, when and where it happened, the people involved, and
any relevant messages or records, if you can. You do not need to investigate the
matter yourself. The Club will take reports seriously, share information only as
needed to respond, and refer matters to Recreation, an appropriate University
office, or authorities when required. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality.
Good-faith reporting and participation in a review must not lead to retaliation.

## 6. Response and review

The Club may take proportionate club-level steps, such as guidance, a warning, a
corrected match result, or a temporary restriction on an app feature while a
serious safety or security concern is reviewed. We will normally explain the
concern to the affected person and give them a reasonable chance to respond before
deciding a club-level consequence, unless immediate action is needed. We will keep
a record of the decision and the reasons for it.

The Club may restrict participation in its own activities when warranted by a
club-level decision, subject to applicable SFU Recreation policy. Matters affecting
SFU Recreation membership privileges are handled through Recreation's conduct and
discipline process. For a review of a club-level ladder, activity, or app
decision, contact `exec@sfubadminton.com`; contact `sport_clubs@sfu.ca` if the
decision involved club leadership or needs Recreation's attention.

## 7. Acknowledgement and updates

Members and app users are expected to follow this Code. The app will show the
effective date and version. We will notify members of material changes and ask for
renewed acknowledgement when appropriate.
