# 0.9.0 (retroactive)

0.9.0 is a label applied after the fact, on 2026-09-12, to mark the end of August, the busiest month in the project's history. It points at a commit from 2026-08-31. No build ever reported this number, and the tag changed no code. These notes summarise the main features added since [0.8.0](0.8.0.md). They do not list every change.

## For members

- Sign in with a passkey, by default, and one sign-in covers both the member app and the exec console.
- A redesigned app: the Feed as an activity river, a phone-first ladder, a schedule built around the day you play, a new notifications bell, and My stats rebuilt around rating, form and attendance charts, including a look back at a finished season.
- Brackets, scores, standings and your own rating update live, without a reload.
- Tournaments: be told which court you are on and say you are there, check in to every event you entered with one QR scan, enter a doubles event on your own and be paired from the waiting list, and see a draw full screen.
- Challenges can use custom formats (best of X games to Y points), with the club's rules shown on the screen.
- Session reminders go out automatically, at the interval you choose, when you said you are going.
- Email preferences by category, with one-click unsubscribe.
- Report a bug with a screenshot, or send feedback, from the app.
- Every club legal document is linked from a footer.
- Discord: connect your club account with `/link`, get your club roles automatically, pick your own ping roles, see your profile card with `/profile`, and file bugs and feedback with `/bug` and `/feedback`.

## For execs and admins

- Permissions: per-person capabilities with an editor, exec portfolios that narrow the console, baselines that can be created and edited, and a varsity trainer level.
- Tournaments: Court Management runs an event end to end; events can play a round robin and then a knockout, with groups, seeding tiers, random draws within tiers, top seeds that skip round one, a third-place playoff, a per-member event cap, and a draw that can be regenerated before play.
- Fees and finance: entering a tournament records what it costs, fee tiers can price by membership, and execs record expenses and other income against a net position for the term.
- Membership type and per-tournament eligibility, with lapsed members able to return.
- Merge duplicate member accounts, with a review step that flags problems instead of refusing.
- Charts across the console: dashboard, roster, sessions, tournaments, ratings, fees, seasons and audit.
- A new console landing page, rebuilt screens for the roster, member detail, announcements, executives and legal, and data tables that work on a phone.
- Season picking across sessions, tournaments and finances, and seasons stored as a term and a year.
- The console moved to `/admin` on the main site.
- Discord: the bot syncs club roles nightly, relays announcements and confirmed results into channels, creates Discord events for active tournaments, pings before sessions, and logs its actions to an audit channel.
- Inactive accounts follow a lifecycle that ends in a purge after 365 days.
