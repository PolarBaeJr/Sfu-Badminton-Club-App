# Release 1.1.0

1.1.0 is the membership release. Members can pay and prove club fees on a new Membership page, sign in with a password, join club events that are not tournaments, and get a short guided tour on their first visit. Execs get fee tools that settle a pasted list of who paid, per-feature switches for member pages, an audit export, and a read-only Data API service for approved consumers.

Compared with production, which runs 1.0.5.

## For members

### Sign-in and account

- Sign-in (`/login`) and sign-up (`/signup`) are separate pages, and sign-in never creates an account.
- Sign up with an email and a password: the password is saved once the emailed code confirms the address.
- Sign in with your password, reset it at `/login/forgot`, or change it in Settings (a change may ask for an emailed confirmation code).
- Email codes and passkeys still work as before; the code remains what proves you own the inbox.
- A second code request inside a minute now says a code was just sent and how long to wait, instead of "Too many attempts".
- Sign out ends only this device; Settings has a new "Sign out other devices".
- Account setup asks one question per screen, in four steps.
- Photo and video consent: off by default, set or withdrawn any time in Settings.
- Signed-out visitors see both Sign in and Join the club, and no longer see Challenge buttons.
- New members now give a last name and answer "Which events do you play in tournaments?" (Men's, Women's or Open events only) to finish account setup, on the web and in Discord.
- Join the club from Discord with `/signup`: the same questions and documents as the web, then an emailed code. The new account is connected to that Discord account.

### Feed and navigation

- The Feed is the home page: a week strip, the month calendar, "Up next" (sessions, club events and tournaments for the next two weeks, the rest under Show more), club activity and a "You" card.
- `/sessions` now opens the Feed; old session links still land on the right card.
- The top bar follows the phone tab bar's order: Feed, Leaderboard, Challenges, Events, My stats, then Membership.
- Top bar menus open on hover with a mouse; phones keep tap-to-open.
- On a phone, Events opens a small menu for Tournaments or Club events, and a sideways swipe moves between them.
- A Discord link on every page (a nav item when signed out, an icon beside the bell when signed in).
- Dropdowns are custom and searchable, controls have rounded corners, and text selection is a neutral tint.
- The footer sits at the bottom of short pages.
- Error screens and error toasts show a code (for example `FEE-104`) with a Copy button, to quote when reporting a problem.

### Tour

- New members get a six-step tour on their first Feed visit; it opens each page it talks about, including a stop at Challenges.
- The tour shows where membership and fees live, and names events and tournaments on the calendar step.
- Browser Back ends the tour, and Settings can replay it.

### Challenges

- The Challenges page is redesigned: a prominent New challenge button, one compact row per challenge, anything waiting on you first, archived challenges folded away.
- Best of 5 and best of 7 results can now be reported in full.
- A game is played to at most 30 points, and deuce caps at the target plus 9; each score is checked before it is submitted.
- Send a challenge with `/challenge send` and report a result with `/challenge report` in Discord. The bot replies only to you, nobody is pinged, and the other side confirms the result on the website. A result reported from Discord records how long the match took (required, 1 to 300 minutes).

### Tournaments and events

- Club events: socials, workshops, clinics, outings and the AGM, with sign-up and withdrawal on their own page.
- Signing up for a club event needs your current waiver; a cancelled event notifies everyone signed up.
- Club events also appear in the Discord Events tab and the calendar (ICS) feed.
- "Past results" is gone from Tournaments, and "You are in" is now "Current tournaments".
- On a phone the draw reads as a collapsible list of rounds, opened on the round in play.
- Your own record so far (played, won, lost, games, points, last match, and rating change on singles) shows on the event page.
- Internal means this season's club fee is paid; an unpaid member is told why they cannot enter an internal event and where to pay.
- Draft tournaments are hidden from members everywhere.

### Membership and fees

- A public Membership page shows this season's prices and a button to buy membership on SFU Recreation.
- Your fee statement moves from `/fees` to Membership (the old link redirects).
- Send a receipt for your fees: a screenshot plus the reference, read automatically where possible, for an e-Transfer or an SFU Rec purchase.
- The Feed shows a banner when fees are unpaid, and profiles can show a Paid badge.
- A public Socials page and a socials row in the footer.

### My stats

- Every match-derived card (match table, recent form, rating line, head-to-head, partners) is now scoped to the chosen season.
- The season picker lists only seasons you played in and always shows which season the page covers.

### Externals

- Non-members ("externals") can sign the liability waiver at `/guest-waiver` without an account and get a proof page to show at the door. Off until the club switches it on.

### Added before release

- A What's new page (`/whats-new`) lists what changed in each version of the app. It is under Settings > About and in the footer, and it can be read without signing in.
- Joining from Discord asks for your SFU email if you have one.

## For execs and admins

- Accounts and Settings are redesigned; Settings shows passkeys as a device list and a Sign-in card.
- Console passkeys and member-site passkeys are separate: only a passkey enrolled in the console opens the console or passes its step-up.
- A password alone never opens the console, and a password-only session cannot enrol a console passkey. A new exec signs in with an email code and adds a console passkey there.
- The top bar is grouped into menus (Dashboard, Play, Events, Members, Club, System, Settings) that open on hover.
- A first-visit console tour, filtered to what each exec can do; replay it from Settings.
- Club events: create, publish, edit and cancel (admin only, seven `events.*` capabilities). An event with sign-ups can only be cancelled, not deleted.
- Club links card: membership prices and the SFU Recreation link, and the club's socials.
- Fees: Submitted and Outstanding tabs, receipt review (method shown, or "Unclear" for the exec to pick), reminders, and a dues badge on a member's page.
- "Add a name" takes an optional email; the payment moves onto the account when that person signs up with it. The amount is now required.
- "Paste a list" on `/fees`: paste emails or names, review what will be marked, settle ambiguous lines and duplicate named payments, then confirm. Only an email or an exact full name marks anybody.
- The bulk Mark Paid dialogs say what they will actually charge when an entrant has no recorded price.
- Tournaments: a live strip above every event tab shows what is on court and what to call next; the phone draw is a list of rounds.
- Tournament fee totals price unrecorded entries by each member's effective group.
- The match entry dialog is cleaner (singles or doubles toggle, a small score table).
- Matches page gains a season picker; voiding a past-season match no longer moves this season's ladder.
- Audit: download the trail as CSV (console edits, sign-ins, tournament actions, notifications), with sign-ins behind their own capability.
- Legal: External waivers (every signing, searchable by name or version), Media consent list, and a Member signatures page showing who signed which version of each document.
- Announcements: when a post is being edited, Delete sits in the composer.
- Discord composer previews both message shapes from the first keystroke.
- Data API keys, minted in Accounts, now have a service that answers them (see below).
- A Member app link in the console's top bar takes you back to the members' site. The console's Settings page links to What's new.
- The audit log's search, filters and download sit in two tidy rows, and the activity chart is gone.
- The four Legal pages are one Legal entry in the Club menu, which opens to show them.
- The Data API has a public changelog page.
- A Data API key can be minted with no expiry, and its date uses the club calendar.
- The Accounts side menu follows the section you are reading.

## Under the hood

### Security and auth

- Sign-in sends email codes with account creation turned off, and Google sign-in on `/login` refuses an address with no member row.
- Every sign-out is scoped; tests fail on any unscoped sign-out.
- Guest (external) waiver signings live in their own append-only table, written only by a service-role function with per-email and per-network throttles. They never appear in member lists, ratings, dues or the bot.
- Fee receipt screenshots sit in a private bucket and are erased by the deletion purge; the member data export covers them.
- Standardized error codes (`AREA-NNN`) come from one registry in `packages/shared`, listed in `packages/shared/ERROR-CODES.md`. Each occurrence carries a reference that can be found in the server logs.
- Carried from the production hotfix: a fresh code is no longer reported as expired.

### APIs and services

- Data API (`apps/data-api`): a zero-dependency Node service serving `/health`, `/v1/players`, `/v1/players/{ref}` and `/v1/matches` through PostgREST as the `data_api_reader` role, never the service key. Keys are hashed, revocation lands within 30 seconds, and logs never hold a key. `season` is always `null` for now (see the README).
- Discord bot: new `/socials`, `/challenge` and `/signup` commands, and club events synced to the Discord Events tab by a shared scheduled-event engine.
- The ICS feed carries club events and honours the sessions and events switches.
- The Data API serves `/changelog` (and `/changelog/`): keyless, not rate-limited by key, GET and HEAD only, under the same stylesheet and CSP as `/documentations`. `/health` still reports 0.1.0.
- The `/signup` modal's email placeholder asks for an SFU email. A placeholder lives in the modal the bot sends, not in the registered command, so no command registration is needed for it.
- The shared top-bar menu can fold rows under a parent row (`nest` on a nav group, `nestItems()` in `packages/ui`). The console's list of record, `NAV_SECTIONS`, is unchanged.
- `Tabs` has a `subtle` variant, and `SeasonSelect` takes a `className`. Every other caller looks as it did.

### Notable fixes

- Member merges work again (00242).
- Voiding an old match no longer shifts the current ladder (00249).
- My stats no longer mixes seasons.

- The sign-in page backdrop renders above the page background.
- Operator scripts: a read-only sign-in log export, and staging snapshot fixes (not part of the app).

## Before you deploy / owner steps

1. **Apply the migrations in numeric order**, 00242 to 00261. Hard dependencies: 00244 needs 00242, 00245 needs 00244, 00248 needs 00247, 00255 needs 00254, and 00256 must come after 00254 (its lists are 00254's plus two). 00257 is safe before or after the code deploy. 00256 and 00258 have no `BEGIN`/`COMMIT` of their own.
2. **Until 00243, 00247 and 00256 are applied**, only admins reach switched-off pages, and saving a new capability grant is refused by the database.
3. **GoTrue, password sign-in:** set `GOTRUE_PASSWORD_MIN_LENGTH=10` on each host so it matches the app's `PASSWORD_MIN_LENGTH`.
4. **GoTrue, reauthentication email:** point `GOTRUE_MAILER_TEMPLATES_REAUTHENTICATION` at `https://sfubadminton.com/email/reauthentication.html`, like the other three templates.
5. **GoTrue, rate-limit header:** check whether password sign-in needs GoTrue's rate-limit header setting on each host. Nothing in the repo records this; confirm before relying on it.
6. **Discord:** after 00280 and 00281 are applied (step 12), run `npm run register -w bot` once so `/socials`, `/challenge` and `/signup` appear (unless `REGISTER_COMMANDS_ON_BOOT=true` is set on the production bot). A deploy does not register commands.
7. **External waivers:** the `guest_waivers` switch ships off. Republish the privacy policy to cover externals before turning it on. Optionally set `GUEST_WAIVER_IP_SALT` on the player app (unset only disables the per-network throttle).
8. **Data API service:** needs 00241 (already on production) and 00258. Mint a `DATA_API_DB_JWT` per environment with the recipe in `apps/data-api/README.md`, and give the service `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` and `DATA_API_DB_JWT` as secret references by name, with `proxy.health: /health`. The token expires (one year in the recipe), so diarise the renewal.
9. **Season rollover (00261):** from the next 09:10 UTC run, an ended active season with a started successor rolls over with the `carry` policy. To pause it, set `auto_rollover_enabled` to false in the `season_settings` platform setting.
10. **Internal entry rule (00260):** after applying, members without paid dues for the season enter tournaments as external. Check that this season's dues are recorded before opening internal events.
11. **Receipts:** set the club's e-Transfer email in the console; an empty address blocks receipt uploads on the Membership page.
12. **Discord challenges and sign-up:** apply 00280, then 00281 (neither has a `BEGIN`/`COMMIT` of its own).
13. **GoTrue, per-IP rate limits:** every Discord sign-up asks GoTrue for its code from the player app's address, not the member's. Check the per-IP email and verify limits on each host so a busy evening of `/signup` is not refused as one client.

## Known limitations

- Data API responses always carry `season: null`, there is no ratings-history route, and rate limits are per process behind the edge limit.
- Sign-in on `/login` now reveals whether an email has an account (the console already did).
- A Google sign-in refused on `/login` leaves an auth user with no member row, on purpose, so `/signup` can continue with it.
- The season rollover never leaves the club with no active season: the year's last season waits until the next one is created and has started.
- Paying dues after entering does not re-price an entry; the fee is snapshotted at entry.
- The external waiver sends no email, and a signing is never merged into a member account.
- A Discord sign-up cannot set up a passkey; the member adds one on the website.
- A member paused for inactivity opens the website once before `/challenge` works for them again.
- Cancelling a Discord sign-up after the code was sent deletes the answers but leaves an unconfirmed auth user, which cannot sign in.
